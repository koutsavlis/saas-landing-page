How does the contact management feature help me keep track of clients and partners?

Store every client and partner in one place, with notes, files and the full history of your conversations. Smart reminders tell you when it's time to follow up, so no relationship falls through the cracks.

Can I customize the dashboards and reporting feature to display the metrics that are most important to my business?

Yes. Add the widgets you need, choose which KPIs to display and save different views for different teams. You can export reports to PDF or CSV, or schedule them to arrive in your inbox every week.

How does the task management feature help me delegate tasks to my team and track progress?

Create tasks, assign them to teammates and set due dates and priorities. Everyone sees what they own, and you can follow progress on a board or timeline view without chasing status updates.

Can I collaborate with my team in real-time using all tools?

Yes. Comments, mentions and file sharing work across every tool, and changes appear instantly for the whole team, so nobody works on an outdated version.

Is the app available on all devices?

Growth runs in any modern browser and has apps for iOS and Android. Your data syncs automatically, so you can pick up where you left off on any device.

How does the app help me stay compliant when working with freelancers and contractors?

Store contracts, track agreements and keep a clear record of who did what and when. Role-based permissions make sure freelancers only see the projects they work on.

Can I switch plans or cancel at any time?

Yes. You can upgrade, downgrade or cancel from your account settings at any time. Changes apply from the next billing cycle and there are no cancellation fees.

Is there a free trial of the Premium plan?

Yes, Premium comes with a 14-day free trial and no credit card is needed. If you don't upgrade, you simply stay on the free Simple plan.
